Mike Shannon

Songwriter · Composer · Musician

The Marveltonez · United Kingdom

Mike Shannon is a UK songwriter, composer and musician, and one half of The Marveltonez songwriting partnership with Mike Blake. Together, Mike Shannon and Mike Blake write commercially focused songs for artists and the international music industry, combining songwriting craft, melody, structure and extensive real-world musical experience.
