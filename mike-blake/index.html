Mike Blake

Songwriter · Producer · Composer

The Marveltonez · Austria

Mike Blake is an English songwriter, producer, composer, arranger, programmer, guitarist, vocalist and musician based in Austria. He is one half of The Marveltonez songwriting partnership with Mike Shannon, writing commercially focused songs for artists and the international music industry.

Songs with hooks. Built for artists.
